# SOST Research Summary v1.1.0

SOST proves a complete all-order classification for the monochromatic-triangle toggle on labeled complete graphs.

For every `n>=6`:

`G~H iff P(G)=P(H) and R(G)=R(H)`,

where `P` is the vertex degree-parity vector and `R=|E| mod 3`.

Thus:

`|Q_n|=3*2^(n-1)`

and:

`Q_(n+1) ~= Q_n x {0,1}`.

The threshold is sharp: `(P,R)` is incomplete at orders four and five and becomes permanently complete at order six.

The proof is computer-assisted only through the exact `K_6` theorem base and is symbolic thereafter. The finite base is accompanied by a standalone spanning-forest certificate covering all `32,768` states. A separate exact `K_7` flood fill checks `2,097,152` states without entering the proof dependency chain.

The broader SOST framework records finite quotient transport, branching coherence, complete-summary interfaces, and an explicit separation between finite discovery evidence and all-order proof. The framework does not claim universal stabilization for arbitrary reversible systems.
