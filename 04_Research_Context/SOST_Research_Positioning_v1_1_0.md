# SOST Research Positioning v1.1.0

The principal theorem lies near several established mathematical categories while using a distinct state-dependent move law.

## Monochromatic structure in edge-colored complete graphs

The dynamics acts on two-colorings of complete-graph edges and permits a move only on a monochromatic triangle. Existence and extremal questions for monochromatic triangles are adjacent context, but they do not by themselves determine connected components of the reversible move graph.

## Constrained graph reconfiguration

Reconfiguration theory studies connectedness under local moves subject to legality constraints. SOST belongs naturally to this broad category. Its primitive operation is the involution `000 <-> 111` on one induced triangle, with legality depending on the current state of that triangle.

## Switching and complementation dynamics

Graph transformation theory contains many operations that complement cuts, neighborhoods, or prescribed induced structures. The SOST move differs by complementing exactly three triangle edges and only in the two monochromatic states.

## Binary invariants and finite-state toggle systems

Degree parity is a natural binary graph invariant, while edge count modulo three supplies an independent grading. The theorem is stronger than invariance: for every `n>=6`, these two quantities are complete orbit coordinates.

## Finite partition, certification, and synthesis

Finite exact orbit partitions, witness-guided refinement, and finite abstraction are established methodological categories. SOST uses finite exact computation only for the order-six theorem base and requires a separate symbolic argument for arbitrary order.

## Focused comparison result

A focused comparison audit across the adjacent categories above did not identify a direct match for the complete combination:

`conditional monochromatic-triangle toggle`

`complete invariant (degree parity, edge count mod 3)`

`sharp permanent threshold n=6`

`|Q_n|=3*2^(n-1)`

`Q_(n+1) ~= Q_n x {0,1}`.

This is a strong novelty signal, not an assertion of absolute historical priority.

The mathematical proof was developed independently of literature comparison. The repository keeps comparison category-level and non-bibliographic.
